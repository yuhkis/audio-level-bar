# Code of conduct

**English** | [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md)

This project aims to be a place where people can collaborate safely, whatever their technical standing, experience, disability, language, or cultural background.

- Discuss code, design, and verifiable behavior — not people
- Respect the range of situations people work in, including users of assistive technology and speech recognition
- Do not share personal information, audio, device logs, or undisclosed vulnerabilities without the consent of the person concerned
- Insults, harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disruption are not accepted

If a problem arises, contact the repository maintainer (@yuhkis on GitHub) privately rather than spreading information about those involved in a public issue. The maintainer will respond as the situation calls for, which may include editing or removing posts and restricting participation.
